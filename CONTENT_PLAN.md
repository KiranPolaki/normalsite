## Rules that matter more than the copy

- **First two lines carry the post.** LinkedIn truncates everything after them.
- **Link in the first comment,** not the body — the case study URL belongs there.
- **Two images beat one.** Native carousels outperform single screenshots; lead with the
  dashboard-style shot, follow with the detail shot.
- **One question per week, maximum.** Ending every post with a question reads as engagement
  bait; ending one with a real one ("how are you capping AI spend?") gets replies.
- **Tweets are not post summaries.** Each line in the X list stands alone; post them on the
  off-days rather than mirroring LinkedIn.
- **Never post two architecture-heavy posts back to back.** The rotation above already
  alternates register — keep it that way if you reshuffle.
